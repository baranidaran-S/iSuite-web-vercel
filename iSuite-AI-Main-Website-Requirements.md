# 19. How It Works Page

## Complete Journey

1. Customer clicks an ad or starts a chat
2. Lead enters through form or messaging channel
3. Assistant responds in the customer’s language
4. Assistant asks qualifying questions
5. Answers are saved
6. Appointment can be booked
7. Conversation can be assigned to team
8. Contact and deal are managed in CRM
9. Follow-ups are scheduled
10. Deal moves through pipeline
11. Won/lost reason is recorded
12. Relevant ad and sales information appears in reports
13. Qualified leads and won deals can be sent back to Meta through Conversions API where configured

# 34. Final Website Story

The website should communicate:

> Businesses receive enquiries from different channels.  
> iSuite AI brings those enquiries into one place.  
> The AI sales assistant responds, qualifies, books and follows up.  
> Every lead becomes a contact and deal.  
> The team manages the sales process through a shared system.  
> Meta ad information helps the business understand where enquiries come from.  
> Reports and permissions help the business operate with visibility and control.

The website must be complete, clear, honest and product-led.
