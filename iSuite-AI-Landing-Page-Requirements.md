# iSuite AI — Landing Page Requirements

**Product:** iSuite AI  
**Company:** MnT Future  
**Purpose:** Focused conversion page for demo and business enquiries  
**Source of Truth:** iSuite AI Product Guide v1.0  
**Positioning:** AI Sales System — not SaaS, not merely a chatbot

---

## 1. Landing Page Goal

The landing page should:

- Explain iSuite AI quickly
- Highlight the main business problems
- Show the core value of the product
- Build trust
- Generate demo/contact enquiries

The landing page must **not** contain every product feature in full detail.

## 2. Primary CTA

**Book a Demo**

Secondary CTA:

- See How It Works
- Talk to Our Team

Use one consistent primary CTA across the page.

---

## 3. Target Audience

- Businesses running Meta ads
- Businesses receiving enquiries through WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger or websites
- Businesses selling through conversations
- Businesses with sales or enquiry-handling teams
- Teams of approximately 2–30 people

Examples:

- Showrooms
- Clinics
- Salons
- Studios
- Schools
- Agencies
- Consultants
- Service businesses

---

## 9. How It Works

### Heading

> From Enquiry to Sales Follow-up — One Connected Journey.

### Steps

#### 1. Customer Enquires

A customer clicks a Meta ad, submits a form or starts a chat.

#### 2. AI Responds

The assistant responds using the business’s approved information and configured tone.

#### 3. Lead Gets Qualified

The assistant asks required questions and saves the answers.

#### 4. Appointment Can Be Booked

The assistant checks configured availability and helps with booking.

#### 5. Team Takes Over

The team sees the conversation history, deal stage, owner and next follow-up.

#### 6. Business Tracks Progress

The business can review contacts, deals, bookings, ad information and sales activity.

---

## 10. Core Benefits

Use benefits rather than listing every small feature.

### Benefit 1 — One Shared Inbox

> Manage WhatsApp Business, Instagram DMs, Facebook Messenger and website chat from one place.

### Benefit 2 — AI Sales Assistance

> Respond in the customer’s language, qualify enquiries, book appointments and follow up based on business rules.

### Benefit 3 — Clear Sales Pipeline

> Turn enquiries into contacts and deals, assign owners and track progress through custom stages.

### Benefit 4 — Better Follow-up Visibility

> Track due and overdue follow-ups so important enquiries do not depend only on memory.

### Benefit 5 — Connected Meta Ads Information

> Understand relevant ad spend, clicks, leads, won deals and deal value where the data is available.

### Benefit 6 — Team Control

> Manage roles, permissions, assignments and AI action history.

- Complete 13-feature documentation
- Full automation trigger/action list
- Full permissions matrix
- Detailed product limitations list
- Full chat-commerce explanation
- Long blog/resource sections
- Technical architecture
- Full pricing table
- Every dashboard metric

- Unapproved customer proof

2. Who it is for
